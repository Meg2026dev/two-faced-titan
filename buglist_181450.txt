STATIC REVIEW FINDINGS:

[MEDIUM] ctx.roundRect() browser compatibility risk
- Location: index.html:~747 (drawPlayer function)
- Explanation: CanvasRenderingContext2D.roundRect() is relatively new (Chrome 99+, Firefox 112+, Safari 16.4+). Older mobile browsers (Android WebView <99, iOS Safari <16.4) will throw TypeError: ctx.roundRect is not a function, crashing the draw loop every frame and freezing the game on those devices.
- Fix approach: Replace with manual moveTo/lineTo/arc path construction, or add a feature-detect polyfill before use.

[LOW] Alert() in Credits blocks main thread and is unstyled
- Location: index.html (buildSettingsButtons credits action)
- Explanation: window.alert() freezes the entire JS event loop until dismissed; also cannot be styled or themed, breaking visual consistency. Acceptable for jam scope but not ideal UX.
- Fix approach: Replace with in-canvas credits overlay (non-blocking). LOW priority — works correctly, just not elegant.

[LOW] Music interval timing drift over long sessions
- Location: index.html (startMusic setInterval 125ms)
- Explanation: setInterval is not sample-accurate; over many minutes the music sequencer can drift slightly from visual/gameplay sync. Not noticeable in short jam sessions.
- Fix approach: Use AudioContext.currentTime-based lookahead scheduler instead of setInterval. LOW — acceptable for 2-5 min sessions.
