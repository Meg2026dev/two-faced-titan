**Concept A — Orchestral-Electronic Hybrid (SELECTED)**

**Concept C — Dark Ambient Ritual**

**Selection:** Concept A — best balance of epic spectacle, rhythmic combat readability, mobile-friendly mix, and theme-resonant metallic textures.
