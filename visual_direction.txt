MOOD: Dramatic colosseum spectacle, readable combat theater. Torch-lit ancient arena meets clean modern arcade UI. Boss feels monumental (fills 40% screen height), player feels agile (small, quick, high-contrast blue vs purple bg). Instant HEADS/GOLD vs TAILS/SILVER read even at glance.

KEY ELEMENTS:
- Titan body: giant circle (coin), embossed inner rim, side-dependent face (HEADS: serene king face + glowing rune forehead circle; TAILS: heraldic shield backplate + rivet dots). 0.4s Y-axis flip with motion-arc trails + brief squash-stretch at land.
- Player: small knight token (blue #4169E1 armor), 2-frame bob idle, lunge-strike lean, dodge-roll rotation, brief i-frame flicker after hit.
- Arena floor: giant coin-medallion (embossed heads/tails halves), slow rotation Zone 2+, splits into rotating quarter-sectors Zone 2, edge crumble Zone 3.
- Danger telegraphs: translucent fill (red/amber, 30% alpha) + pulsing solid outline circle/line. Fill opacity ramps 10%→50% during windup.
- Particles: gold spark burst (hit), silver deflect sparks, coin-shower confetti (MINT BREAK), dust puff (dodge), amber ember drift (ambient), zone-transition fog wipe.
- HUD: chunky vector hearts, 5-segment Mint Combo coin bar (gold fill, empty silver outline), two-sided titan HP coin gauge (gold/silver halves drain from center), score with drop shadow, zone badge.
- Background: silhouetted colosseum arches (darker purple), floating idle coins (parallax drift), torch glow flicker (radial gradient pulse).

TECHNICAL NOTES: Canvas 2D only, all shapes drawn via path commands (no images, no SVG). Internal canvas resolution 800×1200 portrait, CSS scale-to-fit with letterbox. Device pixel ratio aware (crisp lines on retina). Draw order: bg → arena floor → telegraphs → titan → player → particles → popups → HUD (UI last, unshaken by screenshake). Palette shift = lerp bg/edge colors over 1s on zone transition. Atmospheric Effects: 12 floating coin motes (sine drift), torch flicker via time-based alpha, zone fog as low-alpha gradient overlay. Performance: pre-render static bg to offscreen canvas, batch particle fills by color, cap 50 particles.
