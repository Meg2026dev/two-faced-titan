TWO-FACED TITAN
===============

A colossal living coin-face Titan challenges you to a duel of heads and tails!

The Titan perpetually flips between HEADS (vulnerable gold face, glowing rune weak-point) and TAILS (armored silver backplate that launches attacks). TAP to strike during HEADS windows. DRAG to dodge telegraphed slams, projectiles, and lunges during TAILS.

FEATURES:
- 3 escalating Zones with faster flips and deadlier attacks
- MINT COMBO meter — land 5 hits for a massive MINT BREAK
- NEAR-MISS bonuses (+50 pts) for risky late dodges
- Zone bonuses, medals (Bronze/Silver/Gold), high score chase
- Full touch + mouse support (mobile & desktop)
- Procedural Web Audio soundtrack & SFX (music/SFX toggleable)
- Title, How to Play, Pause (music/SFX toggles, resume, back), Settings, Game Over/Victory screens

THEME: Coin Flip (Gameotron Jam)
Controls: Tap to strike, Drag to dodge. Works on smartphone and PC browser.
Genre: Action Boss-Battler / Arcade
